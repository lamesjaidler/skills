# PRD: Shiftswap

Status: raw draft, not yet critiqued · 2026-11-20

## Problem
- Staff at 14 cafés swap shifts in WhatsApp; managers often find out late, or never.
- ~60 swap requests a week.
- ~9 uncovered shifts a month come from swaps.
- Store managers each spend ~3 hours a week fixing rotas.
- Payroll makes ~12 corrections a month.
- Why now: two cafés failed September's working-hours audit; under-18s worked past 10pm after unseen swaps.

## Users
- Hourly staff (~210): offer and take shifts.
- Store managers (14): approve swaps.
- Area managers (2): view only.
- Payroll admin (Mei): pays from approved swaps.

## Goals & success metrics
- Uncovered shifts from swaps: 9 a month to 2 or fewer, three months after launch.
- Manager rota-fix time: 3 hours a week to 1.
- Payroll corrections: 12 a month to 3 or fewer.
- Working-hours breaches from swaps: zero, not negotiable.
- Adoption: 80% of swaps in the tool within 8 weeks.
- Area managers' use: not set (Q1).

## Non-goals
- Sickness cover: HR's absence process.
- Holiday requests: HR's.
- Cross-café swaps: contracts and pay differ per site.
- Manager-posted open shifts: swaps solid first.
- Pay rate changes: everyone keeps their rate.
- Replacing the rota tool: managers like it.

## Requirements
R1. As staff, I want to offer a shift, swap or giveaway, so that a colleague covers it.
R2. As a store manager, I want staff offered only shifts four rules allow, with no override, so that I needn't catch breaches: under-18s not past 10pm; 48-hour week; 11 hours rest; trained for the role.
R3. As a store manager, I want to approve or decline each swap, so that the rota is right.
R4. As staff, I want a swap's status and result, so that I needn't chase.
R5. As staff, I want to be told an unaccepted shift stays mine, so that I turn up.
R6. As a store manager, I want swaps closed 12 hours before the shift, so that late changes come by phone.
R7. As an area manager, I want swap counts per café, so that I spot bad rotas.
R8. As payroll admin, I want a weekly list of approved swaps, so that I pay who worked.
R9. As Head of Operations, I want each swap's offerer, taker, approver and times kept two years, so that an auditor gets them same-day.

## Assumptions & open questions
A1. Managers decide within 4 hours; not asked.
A2. All staff have smartphones; kitchen porters unchecked.
Q1. Area managers' usage target, waiting on Priya (Ops Director).
Q2. Auto-approve on no manager response, waiting on Priya.
Q3. Agency staff in or out, waiting on Tom (HR lead).
Q4. Is a half-covered split shift uncovered, waiting on Mei.

## Risks
- Staff stay on WhatsApp, it's quicker; managers honour no WhatsApp swaps from launch.
- Managers approve without looking.
- Manchester union rep is touchy about tracking hours; talk early.
