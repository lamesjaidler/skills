# PRD: Shiftswap

Status: raw draft, not yet critiqued · 2026-11-20

## Problem
- Staff make about 60 swap requests a week on WhatsApp; managers often find out on the day or never, so shifts go uncovered.
- Why now: in September two cafés failed a working-hours audit: unseen swaps put under-18s past 10pm.

## Users
- Hourly staff (about 210): offer and take shifts.
- Store managers (14, one per café): approve swaps.
- Area managers (2): view only.
- Payroll admin (1): pays from approved swaps.

## Goals & success metrics
- Uncovered shifts from swaps: 9 a month; 2 or fewer three months after launch.
- Manager rota-fixing time: 3 hours a week to 1.
- Payroll corrections: 12 a month to 3 or fewer.
- Working-hours breaches from swaps: zero.
- Swaps made in the product: 80% within 8 weeks.
- Area managers' use: not set (Q2).

## Non-goals
- Sickness cover and holiday requests: HR owns both.
- Swaps between cafés: contracts and pay rates differ per site.
- Manager-posted open shifts: swaps must be solid first.
- Pay rate changes: everyone keeps their own rate.
- Building the rota: the existing tool stays.

## Requirements
R1. As staff, I want to offer a shift as a swap or giveaway, so that a colleague covers it.
R2. As a manager, I want staff offered only shifts that pass four rules, with no override (under-18s off by 10pm; max 48 hours a week; 11 hours' rest; trained for the role, keyholders for opening shifts), so that I needn't catch breaches.
R3. As a manager, I want to approve or decline each accepted swap, so that the rota is right.
R4. As a manager, I want swaps closed 12 hours before the shift, so that late changes come by phone.
R5. As staff in a swap, I want to see its status and be told the result, so that I needn't chase.
R6. As staff, I want to be told an unaccepted shift is still mine, so that I turn up.
R7. As payroll admin, I want a weekly list of approved swaps, so that I pay whoever worked.
R8. As an area manager, I want to see swap counts per café, so that I spot bad rotas.
R9. As Head of Operations, I want each swap's offerer, taker, approver and times kept two years, so that I can answer an auditor the same day.

## Assumptions & open questions
A1. All staff have smartphones; kitchen porters unchecked.
A2. Managers decide within 4 hours; not asked.
Q1. What happens when a manager doesn't respond, waiting on Priya.

## Risks
- Staff stay on WhatsApp; from launch managers honour no WhatsApp swaps.
- Managers approve without looking.
- Manchester union rep is wary of tracking hours; talk early.
